# Release notes

## v0.1.0

Initial release of Winter Window: Snowfall.

- Adds a Snowfall sidebar with drifting flakes and accumulating ground snow.
- Starts snowfall automatically; pause and resume preserve flakes in place.
- Clears gathered ground snow without stopping active snowfall.
- Lets snowfall respond to typing and reveals an aurora after sustained fast typing.
- Adds a decorated Christmas tree drop button.
- Includes the Snowfall Midnight color theme and density and speed settings.
